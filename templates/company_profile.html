About Us

Established in 2008, Eshraq Al-Jazeera has become a leading provider of advanced solutions in telecommunications, low current, information technology, security systems, and intelligent surveillance across Saudi Arabia and the broader Middle East. We are recognized for delivering high-quality services powered by experienced engineers and technical professionals.

Through years of dedication and technical excellence, we have earned the trust of major industry leaders by successfully managing and executing complex, large-scale projects, including high-profile government projects, with precision, agility, and reliability.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable, and future-ready solutions in telecommunications, information technology, low current systems, and security infrastructure. We empower organizations with intelligent, high-performance systems backed by technical excellence and a commitment to customer success.

Our Vision

To be a leading technology provider in the Middle East, known for transforming complex challenges into smart, secure, and scalable solutions across digital infrastructure, low current systems, and intelligent surveillance.

Our Solutions

Information & Communication Technology (ICT)

We build robust ICT infrastructure, including cabling, networking, and server systems, forming the backbone for smart, connected buildings.

CCTV | Closed-Circuit Television

We provide advanced CCTV solutions, from design to installation. Our systems ensure security with high-resolution monitoring and remote access.

Access Control

We deliver secure access control systems using RFID, biometrics, and mobile solutions. We utilize the latest technologies and offer seamless integration.

Fire Alarm Systems

We supply and install fire alarm systems (EN & UL), offering addressable and conventional options. Our solutions ensure rapid detection and reliable evacuation alerts.

Public Address Systems

We implement PA systems for clear announcements and emergency alerts, fully integrated with fire alarms or BMS for real-time communication across all zones.

Building Management System (BMS)

Our BMS solutions centralize control of HVAC, lighting, and safety systems, enhancing efficiency, energy savings, and operational oversight.
